UI/UX Design FAQs

Common questions about our UI/UX design process, deliverables, and how we work with clients.

UX (User Experience) design focuses on the overall feel and functionality of a product—how users interact with it, user flows, and problem-solving. UI (User Interface) design focuses on the visual elements—colors, typography, buttons, icons, and layout. Both disciplines work together to create products that are both beautiful and easy to use. At GlideUps, we provide comprehensive UI/UX services.

A typical UI/UX design project takes 4-8 weeks depending on complexity. This includes research and discovery (1-2 weeks), wireframing (1-2 weeks), UI design (2-3 weeks), and prototyping/testing (1 week). Complex enterprise applications or products requiring extensive user research may take longer. We provide detailed timelines before starting any project.

You receive complete ownership of all design assets including: organized Figma files with components and variants, a comprehensive design system (colors, typography, spacing), wireframes and user flows, interactive prototypes, responsive designs for all breakpoints, icon sets, and complete developer handoff documentation with specs and assets ready for implementation.
