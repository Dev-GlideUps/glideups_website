Why Investing in Social Media is Beneficial

Every business benefits from being where its audience already is — and they're on social media a lot. Globally, the average person spends about "2 hours and 21 minutes a day" scrolling, liking, sharing, and watching content. In some regions, users spend even more time: for example, Latin America averages around "3 hours 32 minutes daily". Think about that — your potential customer checks social networks multiple times a day. Being visible there isn't optional anymore; it's essential.
