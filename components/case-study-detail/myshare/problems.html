What Problems Our Client Facing

Problem

Our client operated multiple NGOs and understood something profound: money alone doesn't solve every problem. Countless people have perfectly usable items sitting in their homes—clothes, furniture, appliances—that they'd happily give to someone in need, but they have no way to find that person. On the other side, individuals facing hardships like medical diagnoses often resort to reaching out to contacts one by one, hoping someone will help. Some respond, many don't.

There was no unified platform where givers and receivers could find each other transparently and with dignity.

Our Solutions

We designed MyShare as a two-sided platform where both sharers and those in need can register and connect meaningfully. Donors can contribute funds directly to verified individuals, ensuring their generosity reaches the right hands. Users can also list items they no longer need—whether it's clothing, electronics, or household goods—making them visible to people who can genuinely use them. Those in need can browse listings, communicate with donors, and book items seamlessly.

We transformed scattered acts of kindness into an organized movement of impact.
