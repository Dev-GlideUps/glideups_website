Idea & planing

Idea & Planning Understanding both sides of the equation was crucial. We mapped the artist's journey—from portfolio building to landing gigs—and the brand's journey—from posting requirements to selecting the right creative. Our architecture addressed pain points on both ends.

Designing & Feedbacks

Designing & Feedback The platform needed to feel inspiring and creative, not corporate. We designed visually rich profile layouts that let artwork speak for itself, while keeping navigation intuitive. Client feedback ensured the experience resonated with real artists and real brands.

Development & Testing

Development & Testing Our team built robust profile systems, job posting workflows, bidding mechanisms, and messaging features. We tested extensively to ensure smooth interactions between artists and brands, and seamless portfolio displays across devices.

Successful Launch

Successful Launch Collabify launched as a refreshing alternative to noisy social platforms—a focused space where talent meets opportunity and creativity thrives.
