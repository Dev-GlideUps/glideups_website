What Problems Our Client Facing

Problem

Our client ran a thriving pods and accessories business—selling flavors, coils, and essential vaping products. But behind the scenes, operations were chaotic. Orders were tracked through registers and spreadsheets. Stock levels required manual counting. Revenue insights only came after hours of reconciliation work.

There was no real-time visibility into the health of the business, and scaling felt impossible when every transaction demanded manual effort.

Our Solutions

We recommended and developed a complete e-commerce ecosystem tailored to their business model. The admin panel gave our client full control—uploading products, managing stock levels, tracking orders, and monitoring revenue in real time. On the customer side, we built a mobile application that enabled wholesalers to browse products and place orders with just a few taps. The result?

A business that once depended on manual processes now runs on automation and transparency. And with the infrastructure in place, our client confidently expanded from serving only wholesalers to reaching everyday consumers directly.
